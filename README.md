## How this edition differs from the general edition

This edition contains the same 479 terms as the general edition, arranged for interpreters who look up Japanese words by their kana:

- Part 1: Japanese → Indonesian, in **gojūon order (あいうえお順)** (pp. 1–19)
- Part 2: Indonesian → Japanese, alphabetical order (A–Z) (pp. 21–36)

General edition (Japanese entries in romaji A–Z order): https://github.com/harmoniam/selayar-maternal-health-glossary — DOI [10.5281/zenodo.24298767](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24298767)

## Scope

Maternal and child health, perinatal care, obstetric emergencies, referral systems, telemedicine, and healthcare financing, with short explanatory notes on selected Japanese healthcare and health insurance concepts.

## Background

Prepared as supporting material for training in Japan and maternal and child health activities under the JICA Partnership Program in Kepulauan Selayar Regency, South Sulawesi, Indonesia, in cooperation with PSD Japan Inc.

## Important notes

This is the first edition (prototype edition), compiled with the assistance of AI from project documents, training materials, and 2026 field research records. Translations depend on context and must be verified before use in clinical decisions or official interpretation of policy. It does not replace clinical guidelines or professional medical interpretation.
